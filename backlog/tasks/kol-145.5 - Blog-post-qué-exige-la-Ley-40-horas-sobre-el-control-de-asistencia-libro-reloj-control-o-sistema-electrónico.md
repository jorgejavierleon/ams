## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Write and publish a Spanish-language blog article explaining the three attendance-registration methods the Ley 40 Horas (Ley 21.561) requires every Chilean employer to use — libro de asistencia, reloj control, or sistema electrónico de registro — and why a paper libro is the weakest of the three in practice. Ties concretely to Kolvi's existing attendance marking feature, which satisfies the "sistema electrónico" option: every mark is server-verified and backed by a signed receipt with folio (Resolución 38, Art. 13). Timely: the jornada dropped from 44 to 42 hours on 2026-04-26, pushing many employers to re-check whether their current registration method still complies. Targets the keyword theme "control de asistencia obligatorio ley 40 horas" / "sistema electrónico de registro de asistencia Chile". Distinct from KOL-145.2 (Resolución 38 incident/rest-day reporting) and KOL-145.3 (overtime calculation) — no topic overlap.
