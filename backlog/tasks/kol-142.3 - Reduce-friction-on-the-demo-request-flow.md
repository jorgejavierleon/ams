## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The only conversion path on the landing page today is a bare email field (DemoRequestForm in resources/js/pages/landing.tsx) with no stated response time and no alternative contact channel. WhatsApp is the default contact channel for Chilean SMB buyers, not email, and giving no reply-time expectation increases the perceived risk of submitting an email to an unfamiliar vendor. Add a stated response-time expectation, and decide with the product owner whether to add a direct scheduling link or WhatsApp CTA alongside the email form.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
