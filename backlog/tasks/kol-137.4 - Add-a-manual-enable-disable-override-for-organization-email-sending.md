## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Platform admins need a manual kill-switch per organization to immediately stop or resume all of its outgoing email, independent of the automatic soft/hard-limit enforcement in KOL-137.3. This lives in the SaaS panel, next to the email volume card (KOL-137.1) and the limit configuration (KOL-137.2), as a toggle/button on the organization page.

Decided semantics (full override, not a conditional one): disabling blocks every outgoing email for that organization immediately, even if the organization is currently well under both its soft and hard limit. Re-enabling resumes sending immediately, even if the organization is still over its hard limit for the current calendar month — the manual switch unconditionally wins over the automatic block in both directions.

Suppression must behave the same way KOL-137.3 specifies for hard-limit suppression: silent from the end user perspective, no 500s, no queued-job failures — the triggering action in the app completes normally even though its email did not go out.

## User stories for manual testing (Gherkin)

```gherkin
Feature: Manually enable or disable an organization's outgoing email

  Scenario: Disabling email blocks sending even under the limits
    Given organization "Acme" is well under both its soft and hard limit this month
    When a platform admin disables email sending for Acme
    And an action in the app would normally trigger an email for Acme
    Then the email is not sent
    And the triggering action still completes without error

  Scenario: Re-enabling resumes sending even if the org is over its hard limit
    Given organization "Acme" is currently blocked after hitting its hard limit this month
    And a platform admin had separately disabled Acme's email sending
    When the platform admin re-enables email sending for Acme
    Then the next email triggered for Acme sends successfully, even though Acme is still over its hard limit

  Scenario: The toggle is visible and reflects current state
    Given organization "Acme" has email sending enabled
    When a platform admin opens Acme's page in the SaaS panel
    Then they see a control showing email sending is enabled, with the option to disable it
```
